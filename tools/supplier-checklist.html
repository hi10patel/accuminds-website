Supplier Verification: Document Collection Checklist

FTA Decision No. 13 of 2026 (issued 22 July 2026) · Article 54(bis), UAE VAT Law · Effective 1 October 2026

From 1 October 2026, VAT-registered businesses must verify each supplier and each supply before deducting input tax. If a supply is connected to tax evasion and the business knew or should have known, the FTA may deny the input tax. Items marked REQUIRED come from the Decision; items marked RECOMMENDED are supporting evidence we advise keeping.

1Supplier file

At onboarding · again if not verified in the last 12 months (Art. 5.1)

Company supplier (Art. 3.1b)

  • Incorporation verified on an official database, or a copy of the certificate of incorporation or trade licence; name, address and employees match REQUIRED
  • ID of the representative (director, agent or employee you deal with): Emirates ID or passport REQUIRED

Individual supplier (Art. 3.1a)

  • Valid ID: Emirates ID or passport REQUIRED
  • Meeting record: met in person or virtually before the supply, with the date REQUIRED

All suppliers

  • Actual place of business verified by electronic means or a site visit, and suitable for the supplier's activity (Art. 3.2): Ejari or tenancy contract, utility bill, dated photo or visit note REQUIRED
  • Risk indicators checked (Art. 3.3): address changed more than twice in 12 months; key employees changed more than twice in 12 months; transactions out of proportion to the supplier's size or history. If any applies, keep a written explanation REQUIRED
  • TRN checked on the FTA portal (dated screenshot) and VAT certificate on file RECOMMENDED
  • Memorandum or incorporation documents showing shareholders and managers RECOMMENDED
  • Official contact details: company-domain email, landline, website; flag personal-email-only suppliers RECOMMENDED

2Transaction file

With every purchase invoice in scope (Art. 4, 5.2)

  • Commercial reason: a general assessment that the supplier's involvement is genuine, e.g. purchase order, contract or accepted quotation (Art. 4.1) REQUIRED
  • Electronic payment: bank transfer advice matching the supplier (Art. 4.2b) REQUIRED
  • Cash payments: only with a documented commercial reason, within legal limits, and easily verifiable (Art. 4.2b) REQUIRED
  • Third-party payment, or an account outside the supplier's country of incorporation: written commercial explanation (Art. 4.2a) REQUIRED
  • Price check: price or margin in line with the market, or the reason it differs, e.g. two comparable quotes (Art. 4.3a) REQUIRED
  • Licence-activity match: the goods or services fall within the supplier's ordinary or licensed activities (Art. 4.3b) REQUIRED
  • Goods: origin and ownership: authenticity and origin of the goods and the supplier's right to sell them, e.g. delivery note or GRN; for imports, bill of lading, packing list and customs declaration (Art. 4.3c) REQUIRED
  • Intermediary rationale: a clear commercial reason for any agent or middleman (Art. 4.3d) REQUIRED
  • Valid tax invoice: supplier TRN, your TRN, invoice number and date, VAT shown separately; needed to deduct input tax under the VAT Law REQUIRED

Thresholds, enhanced checks and your policy

Which supplies this applies to (Art. 6)

Supplies below AED 10,000 (excluding VAT) can be left out of these checks, unless total supplies from that supplier exceed AED 100,000 in the previous 12 months or are expected to in the next 12 months. Then every supply from that supplier is in scope.

Above AED 375,000 from one supplier (previous or next 12 months), the enhanced bank and reputation checks also apply.

Enhanced: supplier above AED 375,000 in 12 months (Art. 3.4)

  • Bank confirmation: a written confirmation from an authorised UAE bank that the supplier holds a bank account, with no reservations or conditions. It need not be addressed to you REQUIRED
    A letter on the supplier's own letterhead is not enough.
  • Reputation review: clients' recommendations where available, and public reviews and media from reliable sources, consistent with the supplier's size and with no signs of tax evasion; dated and saved REQUIRED

Your verification policy (Art. 5.3, 5.4)

  • Written policy naming the people who implement, review and supervise the checks, with their powers and responsibilities REQUIRED
  • Records: document each verification step and keep the supporting documents where the FTA can review them REQUIRED
