PINT AE rules · UAE e-invoicing

Peppol Participant ID for UAE e-invoicing (0235)

IBT-034 and IBT-049, the 0235 scheme and the 10-digit TIN.

Check your invoices free → E-invoicing readiness service

Plain-English summary of the published specification, checked against 19 October 2026 (PINT AE Billing 1.0.4). The specification itself is authoritative.

The short answer

Every e-invoice carries a Peppol electronic address for the seller (IBT-034) and the buyer (IBT-049), each with a scheme (IBT-034-1, IBT-049-1). UAE identifiers use the 0235 scheme: for the seller it is the 10-digit TIN, 1XXXXXXXXX (IBR-148-AE); for the buyer it is the TIN or a predefined identifier, 9XXXXXXXXX (IBR-135-AE, IBR-149-AE). Both are mandatory in PINT AE, and your Accredited Service Provider usually registers them during onboarding.

WarningChecker rule AE-003

Supplier Participant ID (Peppol endpoint) present in scheme:identifier format

Official references
PINT AE IBT-034 Seller electronic address + IBT-034-1 scheme; IBR-081, IBR-062, IBR-CL-25 (CEF EAS list); 0235 identifier is the 10-digit TIN 1XXXXXXXXX (IBR-148-AE)
Applies to
All invoices
How to fix
Your Participant ID is registered during ASP onboarding. Store it in the ERP company profile in the format 0235:<TIN>.
Note
Mandatory in PINT AE. Kept as a warning because the Participant ID is usually set up with your Accredited Service Provider rather than held in the ERP export.
WarningChecker rule AE-004

Buyer Participant ID (Peppol endpoint) present in scheme:identifier format for B2B/B2G

Official references
PINT AE IBT-049 Buyer electronic address + IBT-049-1 scheme; IBR-080, IBR-063, IBR-CL-25; 0235 identifiers 1XXXXXXXXX (TIN) or 9XXXXXXXXX (predefined) per IBR-135-AE / IBR-149-AE
Applies to
B2B and B2G invoices
How to fix
Add a Participant ID field to the customer master (format 0235:<TIN>). Your ASP can look it up on the Peppol directory.
Note
Mandatory in PINT AE. Kept as a warning because the Participant ID is usually set up with your Accredited Service Provider rather than held in the ERP export.

Questions

Common questions, answered.

What does a UAE Peppol Participant ID look like?

The scheme 0235 followed by the identifier, written 0235:<TIN>. For a seller the identifier is the 10-digit TIN starting with 1 (IBR-148-AE).

Who sets up my Peppol Participant ID?

It is registered during onboarding with your Accredited Service Provider. Keep it in your accounting system's company profile in the format 0235:<TIN>.
