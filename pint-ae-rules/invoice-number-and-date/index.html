Invoice number and issue date in PINT AE

IBT-001 and IBT-002: unique numbers and valid dates.

Check your invoices free → E-invoicing readiness service

Plain-English summary of the published specification, checked against 19 October 2026 (PINT AE Billing 1.0.4). The specification itself is authoritative.

The short answer

Every invoice and credit note needs an invoice number (IBT-001, IBR-002) that identifies it uniquely, and an issue date (IBT-002, IBR-003) recorded as a valid date. Our checker also flags issue dates in the future, which are usually typing mistakes; that is a data-quality check, not a PINT AE business rule.

The checks

What we check, and how to fix it.

Each check below runs in our free e-invoice readiness checker. Critical checks count towards the readiness score; warnings are listed but carry no weight.

CriticalChecker rule AE-005

Invoice number present

Official references
PINT AE IBT-001 Invoice number; IBR-002
Applies to
All invoices
How to fix
Every invoice and credit note needs a unique sequential number from the ERP. Do not leave it blank on any line.
CriticalChecker rule AE-006

Invoice number unique within the uploaded file (no two different invoices share a number)

Official references
PINT AE IBT-001 Invoice number (definition: a unique identification of the Invoice)
Applies to
All invoices
How to fix
Two different invoices carry the same number. Fix the numbering series in the ERP so each document number is used once.
Note
Checked within the uploaded file only.
CriticalChecker rule AE-007

Invoice issue date present and a valid date

Official references
PINT AE IBT-002 Invoice issue date; IBR-003, IBR-073
Applies to
All invoices
How to fix
Export the issue date as a real date (DD/MM/YYYY or YYYY-MM-DD), not free text.
CriticalChecker rule AE-008

Invoice issue date not in the future

Official references
Data-quality check (not a PINT AE business rule) on IBT-002 Invoice issue date
Applies to
All invoices
How to fix
Correct future-dated invoices. The issue date must be on or before the date the invoice is reported.
Note
Catches mistyped dates; an e-invoice is issued on its actual issue date.

Questions

Common questions, answered.

Can two e-invoices share the same number?

No. The invoice number (IBT-001) is defined as a unique identification of the invoice, so fix the numbering series in your system if a number repeats.

What date format should the issue date use?

A real date rather than free text. Your Accredited Service Provider converts it to the format the e-invoice needs; an export with DD/MM/YYYY or YYYY-MM-DD dates is fine for checking.
