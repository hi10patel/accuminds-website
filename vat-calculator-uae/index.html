The short answer

UAE VAT is 5%. To add VAT, multiply the price by 1.05. To take VAT out of a price that already includes it, divide by 1.05.

  • Add VAT: AED 1,000 × 1.05 = AED 1,050
  • Remove VAT: AED 1,050 ÷ 1.05 = AED 1,000
  • VAT inside a price: the price × 5 ÷ 105
  • Some supplies are 0% or exempt
  • Displayed prices must include VAT
  • VAT can be rounded to the nearest fils

Example: A price of AED 2,625 including VAT is AED 2,500 plus AED 125 of VAT.

Source: Federal Decree-Law No. 8 of 2017, Articles 3, 34 and 38; Cabinet Resolution No. 52 of 2017, Article 61.

The law

How UAE VAT is worked out.

Federal Decree-Law No. 8 of 2017 on Value Added Tax and its Executive Regulation, Cabinet Resolution No. 52 of 2017. Checked 12 November 2026 on the UAE Legislation portal.

Standard rate
5% on taxable supplies and imports (Article 3). UAE Legislation: Federal Decree-Law No. 8 of 2017 (opens in a new tab)
Adding VAT
VAT is charged on the value of the supply, so a price before VAT is multiplied by 1.05 (Article 3). UAE Legislation: Federal Decree-Law No. 8 of 2017 (opens in a new tab)
Removing VAT
When the price is paid in cash, the value of the supply is the price less the tax (Article 34). So a price that includes VAT is divided by 1.05, and the VAT inside it is 5/105 of the price. UAE Legislation: Federal Decree-Law No. 8 of 2017 (opens in a new tab)
Displayed prices
Declared prices for taxable supplies must include VAT. The Executive Regulation sets the cases where they need not (Article 38). UAE Legislation: Federal Decree-Law No. 8 of 2017 (opens in a new tab)
Zero-rated (0%)
Among others: exports, international transport, the first supply of a new residential building within three years of completion, certain education, preventive and basic healthcare, crude oil and natural gas, and investment precious metals (Article 45). UAE Legislation: Federal Decree-Law No. 8 of 2017 (opens in a new tab)
Exempt
Financial services set out in the Executive Regulation, residential buildings other than the zero-rated first supply, bare land and local passenger transport (Article 46). UAE Legislation: Federal Decree-Law No. 8 of 2017 (opens in a new tab)
Rounding
If the tax on a supply works out to a fraction of a fils, it may be rounded to the nearest fils (Article 61). UAE Legislation: Cabinet Resolution No. 52 of 2017 (opens in a new tab)
VAT to pay for a period
The output tax for the tax period less the recoverable input tax for the same period (Article 53). Input tax is recoverable on goods and services used to make taxable supplies (Article 54). UAE Legislation: Federal Decree-Law No. 8 of 2017 (opens in a new tab)
Tax period
The standard tax period is three calendar months, unless the FTA assigns a different one (Article 62). UAE Legislation: Cabinet Resolution No. 52 of 2017 (opens in a new tab)
Simplified tax invoice
Allowed when the customer is not VAT-registered, or is registered and the amount charged is AED 10,000 or less (Article 59). UAE Legislation: Cabinet Resolution No. 52 of 2017 (opens in a new tab)

Worked example

One invoice, then one quarter.

An invoice for AED 2,500 before VAT, and a quarter with AED 200,000 of sales and AED 120,000 of purchases, all at 5%.

  1. Add VAT to the invoice

    AED 2,500 × 5% = AED 125 of VAT. The customer pays AED 2,625.

  2. Check it backwards

    AED 2,625 ÷ 1.05 = AED 2,500 before VAT, so the VAT inside the price is AED 125.

  3. Output VAT for the quarter

    AED 200,000 of sales × 5% = AED 10,000.

  4. Input VAT for the quarter

    AED 120,000 of purchases × 5% = AED 6,000.

  5. VAT to pay

    AED 10,000 − AED 6,000 = AED 4,000.

Questions

Common questions, answered.

What is the VAT rate in the UAE?

The standard rate is 5%. Some supplies, such as exports, are zero-rated (0%), and a few, such as bare land and local passenger transport, are exempt.

How do I add 5% VAT to a price?

Multiply the price by 1.05. AED 1,000 before VAT becomes AED 1,050: AED 1,000 plus AED 50 of VAT.

How do I remove VAT from a price that includes it?

Divide the price by 1.05 to get the amount before VAT. AED 1,050 including VAT is AED 1,000 plus AED 50 of VAT.

Is the VAT 5% of the total price?

No. VAT is 5% of the price before VAT. Inside a price that already includes VAT, the tax is 5/105 of the price, which is about 4.76%.

Do prices in the UAE have to include VAT?

Yes for taxable supplies: declared prices must include the tax (Article 38 of Federal Decree-Law No. 8 of 2017). The Executive Regulation sets the cases where they need not.

What is the difference between zero-rated and exempt?

A zero-rated supply is a taxable supply charged at 0%. An exempt supply carries no VAT at all. Input tax is recoverable on costs used to make taxable supplies (Article 54).

How do I work out the VAT to pay in a return?

Take the output VAT on your sales for the period and subtract the input VAT you can recover on your purchases (Article 53). The standard tax period is three months.

How is VAT rounded?

If the tax on a supply works out to a fraction of a fils, it may be rounded to the nearest fils (Executive Regulation, Article 61).
