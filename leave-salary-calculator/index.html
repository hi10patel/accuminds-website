The law

How annual leave works.

Federal Decree-Law No. 33 of 2021 on labour relations and its Executive Regulation, Cabinet Resolution No. 1 of 2022. Checked 31 October 2026 on the UAE Legislation portal.

How much leave
At least 30 days a year with full pay after one year of service, and 2 days for each month when service is more than six months but less than one year (Article 29(1)). UAE Legislation: Federal Decree-Law No. 33 of 2021 (opens in a new tab)
Leaving part-way through a year
Leave is due for the part of the last year worked, if service ends before the balance is used (Article 29(1)(c)). UAE Legislation: Federal Decree-Law No. 33 of 2021 (opens in a new tab)
Pay while on leave
Full pay: the wage, which is the basic wage plus cash allowances and benefits in kind under the contract (Article 29(1) and (6), and Article 1). UAE Legislation: Federal Decree-Law No. 33 of 2021 (opens in a new tab)
Paid out when you leave
The cash equivalent of the unused balance due by law, calculated on basic salary (Article 19(2)). UAE Legislation: Cabinet Resolution No. 1 of 2022 (opens in a new tab)
Carrying leave forward
Up to half the annual leave can move to the next year, or be paid in cash at the wage earned when it fell due, by agreement with the employer (Article 19(1)). UAE Legislation: Cabinet Resolution No. 1 of 2022 (opens in a new tab)
Timing
The employer sets leave dates around work needs and gives at least one month's notice, and cannot stop leave being taken for more than two years unless the worker chooses to carry it forward or be paid for it (Article 29(4) and (8)). UAE Legislation: Federal Decree-Law No. 33 of 2021 (opens in a new tab)
Part-time work
Leave in proportion to contracted hours, with a minimum of five working days a year (Article 18). UAE Legislation: Cabinet Resolution No. 1 of 2022 (opens in a new tab)
Probation
Leave may be taken from the balance during probation if the employer agrees; a worker who leaves during probation is paid for the rest of the balance (Article 29(3)). UAE Legislation: Federal Decree-Law No. 33 of 2021 (opens in a new tab)

Questions

Common questions, answered.

Is UAE leave salary based on basic or total salary?

It depends when it is paid. During leave you receive full pay, the wage with allowances. Unused leave paid out when your service ends is calculated on basic salary (Executive Regulation, Article 19(2)).

How many days of annual leave do I get in the UAE?

At least 30 days a year after one year of service. Between six months and one year of service, 2 days for each month.

Do I get paid for unused leave if I resign?

Yes. When your service ends, the unused balance due by law is paid in cash on basic salary (Executive Regulation, Article 19(2)).

How is a day of leave worked out in AED?

The law does not set the daily rate. This calculator uses monthly salary divided by 30, the common practice, and also shows salary multiplied by 12 and divided by 365.

Can I carry annual leave forward?

Up to half of it, into the following year, with the employer's agreement. The employer cannot stop you from taking leave for more than two years.

Can my employer choose when I take leave?

Yes, around work needs, but it must tell you the dates at least one month in advance.

Does this apply in DIFC, ADGM or to domestic workers?

No. It follows the federal Labour Law. DIFC and ADGM have their own employment laws, and domestic workers have a separate law.
