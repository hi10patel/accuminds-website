The law

How gratuity is worked out.

Federal Decree-Law No. 33 of 2021 on labour relations and its Executive Regulation, Cabinet Resolution No. 1 of 2022. Checked 28 October 2026 on the UAE Legislation portal.

Who is entitled
Foreign full-time workers who have completed one or more years of continuous service (Article 51(2)). UAE nationals are covered by the pensions and social security law instead (Article 51(1)). UAE Legislation: Federal Decree-Law No. 33 of 2021 (opens in a new tab)
The rate
The basic wage of 21 days for each of the first five years of service, and of 30 days for each year after that (Article 51(2)). UAE Legislation: Federal Decree-Law No. 33 of 2021 (opens in a new tab)
Which wage
The last basic wage received: the wage in the contract, without allowances or benefits in kind (Article 51(5) and Article 1). UAE Legislation: Federal Decree-Law No. 33 of 2021 (opens in a new tab)
Part years and absence
Parts of a year are paid in proportion once one year of continuous service is completed, and days of unpaid absence do not count as service (Article 51(3) and (4)). UAE Legislation: Federal Decree-Law No. 33 of 2021 (opens in a new tab)
The cap
The total gratuity may not exceed two years' wage (Article 51(6)). UAE Legislation: Federal Decree-Law No. 33 of 2021 (opens in a new tab)
Part-time work
A share of the full-time gratuity: contracted annual hours divided by full-time annual hours. No gratuity under the temporary work model or for service under a year (Article 30). UAE Legislation: Cabinet Resolution No. 1 of 2022 (opens in a new tab)
Deductions
Only amounts due by law or a court judgment, such as loans, overpayments or approved penalties, under the conditions in Article 29. UAE Legislation: Cabinet Resolution No. 1 of 2022 (opens in a new tab)
When it is paid
Within 14 days of the end of the contract, together with wages and other final dues (Article 53). UAE Legislation: Federal Decree-Law No. 33 of 2021 (opens in a new tab)

Worked example

Seven years on AED 8,000 basic.

  1. Daily basic wage

    AED 8,000 ÷ 30 = AED 266.67.

  2. First five years

    5 × 21 days × AED 266.67 = AED 28,000.

  3. Years six and seven

    2 × 30 days × AED 266.67 = AED 16,000.

  4. Total gratuity

    AED 44,000, well under the cap of two years' wage.

Questions

Common questions, answered.

Is UAE gratuity calculated on basic salary or total salary?

On the last basic salary. The Labour Law defines basic wage as the wage in the contract without allowances or benefits in kind, so housing and transport allowances are left out.

Do I get gratuity if I resign?

Yes, once you have completed one year of continuous service. Article 51 of the current Labour Law gives the same formula whether the contract ends by resignation or termination.

What if I worked less than one year?

Full-time foreign workers become entitled after one year of continuous service, so there is no gratuity for less. After that, part years are paid in proportion.

When must my employer pay my gratuity?

Within 14 days of the end of the contract, with your other final dues (Article 53).

Does this apply in free zones?

It follows the federal Labour Law. DIFC and ADGM have their own employment laws, and domestic workers have a separate law, so the calculation can differ there.

What if my employer enrolled me in the savings scheme?

Article 51(8) lets the Cabinet approve alternatives to gratuity, such as the voluntary end-of-service savings scheme. While you are enrolled, the scheme's contributions replace gratuity for that period.

Should employers set aside gratuity every month?

Yes. Gratuity builds up as people work, so accruing it monthly keeps the liability in your accounts and avoids a large one-off cost when someone leaves.
