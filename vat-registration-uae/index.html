VAT registration in the UAE

Check whether you must register for VAT, or can choose to, then see the documents the FTA asks for and the four steps on EmaraTax.

Check if you must register ↓ VAT registration service

The check

Do you have to register?

Amounts in AED, excluding VAT. Your answers stay in your browser; nothing is sent or stored.

Where is the business established?

Taxable supplies are sales charged at 5% or 0%; exempt sales do not count.

The rules

Thresholds, deadline and penalty.

From the FTA's VAT registration pages and its published table of administrative penalties. Checked 31 October 2026.

Must register
A UAE business whose taxable supplies and imports exceeded AED 375,000 over the previous 12 months, or are expected to exceed it in the next 30 days. FTA: Registration for VAT (opens in a new tab)
May register
Below that, a business may register voluntarily once its taxable supplies and imports, or its taxable expenses, exceed AED 187,500 over the previous 12 months or are expected to in the next 30 days. FTA: Registration for VAT (opens in a new tab)
Non-resident businesses
No threshold: registration is mandatory for taxable supplies in the UAE unless someone else in the UAE is responsible for the VAT on them. FTA: VAT registration (opens in a new tab)
Deadline
Apply within 30 days of becoming required to register. The rules apply to anyone doing business in the UAE, even without a trade licence. FTA: VAT registration (opens in a new tab)
Late registration penalty
AED 10,000 for not applying within the time the law allows. FTA: Cabinet Decision No. 40 of 2017 as amended (unofficial translation) (opens in a new tab)
Fee and timing
Registration is free. The application takes about 45 minutes, and the FTA aims to decide within 20 business days of receiving a complete application. FTA: VAT registration (opens in a new tab)
Branches and sole establishments
One TRN covers all branches of a company. All sole establishments of the same owner share one TRN, and their turnover is added together for the threshold. FTA: VAT registration (opens in a new tab)

Documents

What to have ready.

The FTA's list for a company; it varies by legal form. Upload each as a PDF.

  • Company documents

    Certificate of incorporation, memorandum of association or partnership agreement, and the trade licence with any branch licences.

  • Owners and signatories

    Emirates ID and passport copies of the owners and authorised signatories, and a power of attorney if the manager is not named in the memorandum.

  • Turnover declaration

    A signed and stamped letter stating taxable supplies and monthly sales since the business started. The FTA publishes a template.

  • Evidence

    Invoices, purchase orders, contracts or leases that support the turnover. Registering on expenses needs at least five VAT invoices; expected revenue needs signed purchase orders or contracts.

  • Optional and if applicable

    A bank letter in the company's name, and customs details if you import or export.

On EmaraTax

Four steps to a TRN.

Once you have it, customers can confirm it with a TRN verification.

  1. Create an EmaraTax account

    Sign up on the FTA website and activate the account.

  2. Add a taxable person

    From the dashboard, create a new taxable person profile for the business and open it.

  3. Register for VAT

    Click Register under Value Added Tax, complete the form and upload the documents as PDFs (up to 15 MB each).

  4. Get your TRN

    Once approved, the VAT registration certificate with your TRN appears in your EmaraTax dashboard.

Questions

Common questions, answered.

What is the VAT registration threshold in the UAE?

AED 375,000 of taxable supplies and imports for mandatory registration, and AED 187,500 of taxable supplies and imports or taxable expenses for voluntary registration.

How long does VAT registration take?

About 45 minutes to submit online. The FTA aims to decide within 20 business days of receiving a complete application.

Is there a fee to register for VAT?

No. VAT registration through EmaraTax is free.

What is the penalty for late VAT registration?

AED 10,000 if you do not apply within 30 days of becoming required to register.

Can I register for VAT on expenses alone?

Yes, voluntarily, if taxable expenses exceed AED 187,500. The FTA asks for at least five VAT invoices as evidence.

Does each branch need its own TRN?

No. All branches register under the company's single TRN and file one VAT return.

Do free zone companies need to register for VAT?

The same thresholds apply. For a business in a designated zone trading goods, it depends on its activities and supplies, and the FTA may ask for a business flow showing how goods move.

What happens after I register?

You receive a TRN, charge VAT on taxable supplies, issue tax invoices and file VAT returns for each tax period.
