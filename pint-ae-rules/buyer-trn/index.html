PINT AE rules · UAE e-invoicing

Buyer TRN on UAE e-invoices (IBT-048)

Optional for buyers without a TRN, required on reverse charge lines.

Check your invoices free → E-invoicing readiness service

Plain-English summary of the published specification, checked against 19 October 2026 (PINT AE Billing 1.0.4). The specification itself is authoritative.

The short answer

The buyer's TRN goes in IBT-048, the buyer VAT identifier. PINT AE treats it as optional for buyers without a TRN, but when it is given it must follow the TRN format (IBR-132-AE: 15 digits, starting with 1 and ending with 03), and it is required when any line is VAT reverse charge (IBR-103-AE). Under UAE VAT law, a tax invoice to a VAT-registered buyer shows the buyer's TRN.

CriticalChecker rule AE-002

Buyer TRN, when given, is 15 digits starting with 1 and ending with 03; required on reverse charge invoices

Official references
PINT AE IBT-048 Buyer VAT identifier (0..1); IBR-132-AE (TRN format); IBR-103-AE (required on reverse charge lines)
Applies to
B2B and B2G invoices
How to fix
Store each VAT-registered customer's 15-digit TRN (starts with 1, ends with 03) in the customer master and print it on the invoice. It is mandatory when any line is VAT reverse charge.
Note
Optional in PINT AE for buyers without a TRN, so a blank value passes unless a line is reverse charge (AE). Under UAE VAT law a tax invoice to a VAT-registered buyer shows the buyer's TRN.

Questions

Common questions, answered.

Is the buyer TRN mandatory in PINT AE?

Not for buyers without a TRN, but it is required when any invoice line is VAT reverse charge (IBR-103-AE). A tax invoice to a VAT-registered buyer should show the buyer's TRN under UAE VAT law.

Where should the buyer TRN be stored?

In the customer master of your accounting system, so it prints on every invoice to that customer without retyping.
