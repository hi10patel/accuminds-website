QuickBooks and UAE e-invoicing: connecting to an ASP

QuickBooks is not a UAE Accredited Service Provider, so QuickBooks users exchange e-invoices through a separate accredited provider. Here is how to choose one, connect it and get your data ready.

Free readiness check → Book a consultation

Facts checked 22 October 2026 against the Ministry of Finance list and official specifications.

The short answer

QuickBooks Online is sold in the UAE, but Intuit is not on the Ministry of Finance list of eInvoicing Accredited Service Providers, and QuickBooks' UAE pages do not describe built-in e-invoicing. To comply, choose an Accredited Service Provider (ASP) that can take your invoices from QuickBooks, appoint it in EmaraTax, and clean your QuickBooks data so every invoice carries the fields the UAE format (PINT AE) requires.

Where QuickBooks stands

The facts, with sources.

Is QuickBooks an accredited provider?
No. Intuit and QuickBooks do not appear on the Ministry of Finance list of eInvoicing Accredited Service Providers (checked 22 October 2026). Ministry of Finance: eInvoicing Accredited Service Providers (opens in a new tab)
Does QuickBooks have built-in UAE e-invoicing?
Not stated on QuickBooks' UAE website, which describes VAT tracking and invoicing but not Peppol or PINT AE e-invoicing (checked 22 October 2026). QuickBooks UAE (opens in a new tab)
What this means
Your ASP sends and receives the e-invoices. QuickBooks stays your accounting system, connected to the ASP by an integration or a data export, depending on what the ASP offers. Ministry of Finance: eInvoicing Accredited Service Providers (opens in a new tab)

Step by step

From QuickBooks to compliant e-invoices

  1. Check your invoice data

    Export a few recent invoices and credit notes and test them against the PINT AE fields below, or run them through our free readiness checker.

  2. Shortlist ASPs that support QuickBooks

    Pick providers from the Ministry of Finance list and ask each one the questions below. Integration support differs between providers, so get the answers in writing.

  3. Agree the contract, then appoint in EmaraTax

    You appoint your chosen ASP in EmaraTax. The ASP then onboards you and sets up your Peppol participant ID, which for UAE businesses is 0235 followed by your 10-digit TIN.

  4. Fix data at the source

    Update customer TRNs, VAT categories, item descriptions and addresses in QuickBooks itself, so every new invoice is right first time.

  5. Test, then go live

    Send test invoices and credit notes through the ASP, check what they reject, and confirm how incoming supplier e-invoices reach QuickBooks.

Before you go live

Questions to ask an ASP before you sign

  • How do you connect to QuickBooks?

    A direct integration with QuickBooks Online, an upload of exported files, or manual entry in your portal? Which QuickBooks plans are supported?

  • Where do the PINT AE fields come from?

    How are TRNs, VAT category codes (including reverse charge), emirate codes and item descriptions mapped from QuickBooks?

  • Credit notes and foreign currency

    Do credit notes keep the link to the original invoice, and do non-AED invoices carry the VAT total in AED?

  • Incoming e-invoices

    How do supplier e-invoices you receive get into QuickBooks as bills?

  • Pricing and support

    What is the price per invoice or per month, what does onboarding include, and who fixes rejected invoices?

Data checklist

What every e-invoice must carry.

From the UAE e-invoicing specification, PINT AE. These apply whatever software you use. Peppol: PINT AE specific rules (opens in a new tab)

Your TRN
15 digits, starting with 1 and ending with 03, exactly as on your VAT certificate (IBR-132-AE).
Customer TRNs
Stored for VAT-registered customers; mandatory when an invoice line is VAT reverse charge (IBR-103-AE).
VAT category per line
Each line needs a category: S standard, Z zero rated, E exempt, O out of scope, AE reverse charge or N standard rate additional VAT (IBR-139-AE, IBR-145-AE).
Document types
Tax invoices (380), invoices out of scope of tax (480), credit notes (381) and credit notes for goods or services (81). Credit notes must reference the original invoice (IBR-055-AE).
Item name and description
Every line needs both (IBR-025, IBR-125-AE), so short codes alone are not enough.
Foreign-currency invoices
Show the VAT total in AED and the exchange rate when the invoice is not in AED (IBR-159-AE, IBR-175-AE).
Addresses
Seller and buyer addresses need address line 1, city and emirate code: AUH, DXB, SHJ, UAQ, FUJ, AJM or RAK (IBR-128-AE, IBR-143-AE, IBR-144-AE).
Line VAT amounts
VAT is carried per line, and is zero on reverse charge and zero-rated lines (IBR-162-AE, IBR-165-AE).

Deadlines

When you need to be ready.

Revenue of AED 50 million or more
Deadline to appoint an Accredited Service Provider: 30 October 2026 (if it has passed, appoint one now). E-invoicing applies from 1 January 2027. Ministry of Finance: e-invoicing amendments (2026) (opens in a new tab)
Revenue below AED 50 million
Appoint an Accredited Service Provider by 31 March 2027; e-invoicing applies from 1 July 2027. Ministry of Finance: UAE e-invoicing (opens in a new tab)
Tax group transactions
Transactions between members of the same Tax Group are excluded from e-invoicing for 24 months from 1 January 2027 (Ministerial Decision No. 197 of 2026). Ministry of Finance: e-invoicing amendments (19 October 2026) (opens in a new tab)

Questions

Common questions, answered.

Do I have to leave QuickBooks for UAE e-invoicing?

No. You can keep QuickBooks and send e-invoices through an Accredited Service Provider that connects to it. Some businesses move to an accounting system with built-in e-invoicing instead; that is a choice, not a requirement.

Is QuickBooks an Accredited Service Provider in the UAE?

No. Intuit and QuickBooks are not on the Ministry of Finance list of eInvoicing Accredited Service Providers as checked on 22 October 2026.

Is there a QuickBooks Peppol or PINT AE integration for the UAE?

Not from QuickBooks itself: its UAE website does not describe Peppol or PINT AE e-invoicing (checked 22 October 2026). The integration comes from your Accredited Service Provider, which connects to QuickBooks or takes an export of your invoices and sends them in the PINT AE format over Peppol. Ask each provider how it connects to QuickBooks before you sign.

What is my Peppol ID?

Once your ASP onboards you, your Peppol participant ID is 0235 followed by your 10-digit TIN.

When must my business appoint an ASP?

The deadline is 30 October 2026 if your revenue is AED 50 million or more, and 31 March 2027 if it is below that. If your deadline has passed, appoint one as soon as possible.

Do e-invoices have to be stored inside the UAE?

No, not since Ministerial Decision No. 197 of 2026, effective 1 October 2026. You must still keep them, with their data, for the period set by the Tax Procedures Law and provide them to the FTA on request.
