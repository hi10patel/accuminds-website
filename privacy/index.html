Privacy

What this website collects.

In short: what you send us through the contact form, and anonymous visit counts. No cookies, and nothing you type into a calculator. Last updated 12 November 2026.

Who we are
Accuminds Consulting FZCO, DSO – IFZA, IFZA Properties, Dubai Silicon Oasis, Dubai, UAE. For anything on this page, email info@accumindsconsulting.ae.
Contact form
When you send the form we receive your name, your work email, the service you chose and your note. The form is delivered by Formspree, which passes the message to our inbox and keeps a copy in our Formspree account. We use these details to reply to you and to deal with your request.
WhatsApp, email and phone
If you contact us this way we receive what you send, along with your number or email address. WhatsApp messages are handled by WhatsApp under its own terms.
Calculators and checks
They run in your browser. The figures, dates and numbers you enter are not sent to us and are not stored. The e-invoice checker reads your invoice files in your browser and does not upload them.
Counting visits
We count visits with GoatCounter, without cookies. It records the page, the site you came from and your screen width, and works out your browser, operating system and country from the request. It does not store your IP address, and the totals cannot be linked to a person.
Counting clicks
We also count clicks on our contact buttons, downloads, sent contact forms and whether a calculator was used. Only the fact is counted, never what you typed.
Cookies
This site sets no cookies. Your browser keeps a small setting or two on your own device, such as whether you paused the animation. They are not sent to us.
Hosting and fonts
The site is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads its typeface from Google Fonts. Like any web server, both receive your IP address when your browser asks for the pages and fonts.
Sharing
We do not sell your details or pass them to anyone else for marketing.
How long we keep enquiries
For as long as we need them to deal with your request and any work that follows.
Your choices
Ask us at any time to show you, correct or delete the details you have sent us: email info@accumindsconsulting.ae.
Changes
We update this page when what the site does changes. The date at the top shows the latest version.
