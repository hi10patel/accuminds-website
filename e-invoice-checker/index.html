UAE E-Invoice Readiness Checker (PINT AE)

Check a sample of your sales invoices against the PINT AE fields used for UAE e-invoicing. Upload an Excel, CSV or PDF export, match your columns, and get a readiness score, the gaps rule by rule and a PDF report.

  • 27 PINT AE 1.0.4 rules: 25 critical and 2 warnings.
  • Column presets for TallyPrime, Zoho Books, QuickBooks Online, Odoo and SAP Business One.
  • Your file is read inside your browser. Nothing is uploaded, stored or sent.

The checker needs JavaScript. See also UAE e-invoicing readiness and the e-invoicing software guides.
