Questions

Common questions, answered.

What is PINT AE?

The UAE's version of the Peppol International (PINT) billing specification: the data model and business rules every UAE e-invoice is validated against. These pages follow PINT AE Billing 1.0.4.

Who applies these rules to my invoices?

Your Accredited Service Provider validates each e-invoice before it is exchanged, so data that breaks a rule has to be fixed in your accounting system first.

How can I check my invoices against these rules?

Export a few recent invoices from your accounting system and run them through our free e-invoice readiness checker. It reads the file in your browser and lists the gaps, rule by rule.
