TRN verification in the UAE

Check that a TRN has the right format, then confirm it on the FTA website in four steps. Plus when a TRN must appear on a tax invoice.

On the FTA website

Confirm the TRN is registered.

Only the FTA's own verification confirms that a TRN is registered. It is open to anyone, with no EmaraTax login. Open tax.gov.ae (opens in a new tab)

  1. Open TRN/TIN verification

    On tax.gov.ae, it is at the top of the page.

  2. Enter the number

    Choose TRN and enter the 15 digits, or choose TIN for a 10-digit number.

  3. Enter the security code

    Type the code shown in the image and submit.

  4. Compare the result

    It should match the business named on the invoice. If it is not found or the name differs, ask the supplier for its VAT registration certificate before you claim the input VAT.

The rules

When a TRN must be on an invoice.

Cabinet Resolution No. 52 of 2017 (the VAT Executive Regulation, as amended), the PINT AE specification and the Ministry of Finance e-invoicing guidelines. Checked 31 October 2026.

What a TRN is
A unique number the FTA assigns to each person registered for tax (Article 1). UAE Legislation: Cabinet Resolution No. 52 of 2017 (opens in a new tab)
Format
15 digits. On UAE e-invoices a VAT TRN must start with 1 and end with 03, with no spaces, dashes or prefix (rule IBR-132-AE). Peppol: PINT AE specific rules (opens in a new tab)
Full tax invoice
Shows the supplier's name, address and TRN, and the recipient's name, address and TRN if the recipient is VAT registered (Article 59(1)). UAE Legislation: Cabinet Resolution No. 52 of 2017 (opens in a new tab)
Simplified tax invoice
Shows the supplier's TRN only. Allowed when the recipient is not VAT registered, or is registered and the supply is AED 10,000 or less, and reverse charge does not apply (Article 59(2) and (5)). UAE Legislation: Cabinet Resolution No. 52 of 2017 (opens in a new tab)
Where to verify
The TRN/TIN verification on the FTA website: a 15-digit TRN or a 10-digit TIN, plus a security code. No login is needed. FTA website: TRN/TIN verification (opens in a new tab)
TRN and TIN
Your 10-digit TIN is the first 10 digits of your TRN. In a tax group, each member's TIN comes from its own TRN, not the group's. Ministry of Finance: UAE Electronic Invoicing Guidelines v2.0 (26 October 2026) (opens in a new tab)

Before you claim input VAT

A valid TRN is only the first check.

From 1 October 2026, FTA Decision No. 13 of 2026 also asks you to verify the supplier and the supply before you claim input VAT. See which checks apply in the Supplier Verification Check.

Questions

Common questions, answered.

How do I verify a TRN number in the UAE?

On the FTA website (tax.gov.ae), open TRN/TIN verification at the top of the page, choose TRN, enter the 15 digits and the security code, and submit. Check that the result matches the business named on the invoice.

How many digits is a UAE TRN?

15. On UAE e-invoices a VAT TRN must start with 1 and end with 03 (PINT AE rule IBR-132-AE).

Can I check a TRN without logging in to EmaraTax?

Yes. The TRN/TIN verification on the FTA website is open to anyone and needs only the number and the security code.

What is the difference between a TRN and a TIN?

A TRN has 15 digits; the TIN is its first 10 digits (Ministry of Finance e-invoicing guidelines). The FTA's verification accepts either: choose TRN or TIN before you enter the number.

Must my customer's TRN be on my tax invoice?

Yes, if the customer is VAT registered and you issue a full tax invoice (Article 59(1)). A simplified tax invoice shows only your own TRN.

What if the TRN is not found or the name does not match?

Ask the supplier for its VAT registration certificate and a corrected invoice before you claim the input VAT. A tax invoice must show the supplier's TRN (Article 59(1)).

Does this tool check the FTA register?

No. It checks the format only, in your browser, and nothing is sent. Only the FTA's own verification confirms that a TRN is registered.
