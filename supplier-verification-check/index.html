The rule

FTA Decision No. 13 of 2026, in brief.

Issued 22 July 2026, effective 1 October 2026. Source: FTA Decision No. 13 of 2026 (unofficial English translation) (opens in a new tab)

Who and when
VAT-registered businesses, for supplies received from 1 October 2026, before deducting input tax (Art. 2, 7). If a supply is connected to tax evasion and the business knew or should have known, the FTA may deny the input tax (Article 54(bis), VAT Law).
Below AED 10,000
A supply under AED 10,000 excluding VAT can be left out of the checks, unless total supplies from that supplier exceed AED 100,000 in the previous 12 months or are expected to in the next 12 (Art. 6).
Supplier checks
Companies: incorporation verified and the representative's ID. Individuals: valid ID and a meeting, in person or virtually, before the supply. All: actual place of business and risk indicators (Art. 3.1 to 3.3).
Above AED 375,000
A written confirmation from an authorised UAE bank that the supplier has a bank account, and a review of clients' recommendations, public reviews and media (Art. 3.4).
Supply checks
Genuine commercial reason, electronic payment (cash only with a documented reason), explanations for third-party or foreign-account payments, market price, licence-activity match, origin and ownership of goods, and intermediary rationale (Art. 4).
Procedures
Verify a new supplier, and again after 12 months; check every supply in scope; document the steps and keep the records; keep a written policy naming who implements, reviews and supervises (Art. 5).

Questions

Common questions, answered.

Does every supplier need to be verified from 1 October 2026?

Every supplier whose supplies are in scope. Supplies under AED 10,000 excluding VAT can be left out, unless total supplies from that supplier exceed AED 100,000 in the previous or next 12 months.

Is a letter from the supplier confirming its bank account enough?

No. Above AED 375,000, the Decision asks for a written confirmation issued by an authorised bank in the UAE, without reservations or conditions. It does not have to be addressed to you.

How often must suppliers be re-verified?

When you first deal with them, and again on recurring dealings if they have not been verified in the previous 12 months.

What happens if I do not verify?

If a supply turns out to be connected to tax evasion and you knew or should have known, the FTA may deny your input tax deduction under Article 54(bis) of the VAT Law.
