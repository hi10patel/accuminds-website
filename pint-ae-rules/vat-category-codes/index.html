PINT AE rules · UAE e-invoicing

PINT AE VAT category codes: S, Z, E, O, AE and N

Mapping your tax codes to the six categories, with the right rates.

Check your invoices free → E-invoicing readiness service

Plain-English summary of the published specification, checked against 19 October 2026 (PINT AE Billing 1.0.4). The specification itself is authoritative.

The short answer

Every invoice line needs a VAT category code (IBT-151): S standard rated, Z zero rated, E exempt, O out of scope, AE reverse charge and N standard rate additional VAT (IBR-145-AE, IBR-139-AE). The line VAT rate (IBT-152) must match: 5.00 for the standard rate (IBR-190-AE), 0 for zero rated, no rate for exempt and out of scope, a rate for reverse charge, and a non-zero rate for additional VAT (IBR-111-AE).

The checks

What we check, and how to fix it.

CriticalChecker rule AE-017

VAT category code per line (S standard, Z zero, E exempt, O out of scope, AE reverse charge, N standard rate additional VAT)

Official references
PINT AE IBT-151 Invoiced item VAT category code; IBR-SR-58, IBR-145-AE, IBR-139-AE; AE VAT category codes S, Z, E, O, AE, N
Applies to
All invoices
How to fix
Map every ERP tax code to a PINT AE VAT category (S, Z, E or O) and export it per line.
CriticalChecker rule AE-018

VAT rate consistent with the line VAT category

Official references
PINT AE IBT-152 Invoiced item VAT rate; IBR-190-AE (standard rate 5.00), ALIGNED-IBRP-Z-05 (zero rated 0), ALIGNED-IBRP-E-05 / O-05 (no rate for exempt / out of scope), ALIGNED-IBRP-AE-05-AE (rate required for reverse charge), IBR-111-AE (additional VAT rate not zero)
Applies to
All invoices
How to fix
Standard-rated lines must carry 5%; zero-rated, exempt and out-of-scope lines must carry 0%. Correct the tax code set-up in the ERP.
Note
Exempt and out-of-scope lines pass with a blank or 0% rate; PINT AE leaves the rate out, which the Accredited Service Provider mapping handles. Reverse charge and additional VAT lines expect the 5% rate.

Questions

Common questions, answered.

What does VAT category code AE mean on a UAE e-invoice?

Reverse charge. Lines with category AE carry a VAT rate, a zero VAT amount (IBR-162-AE) and require the buyer's TRN (IBR-103-AE).

What is the difference between Z, E and O?

Z is zero rated (rate 0), E is exempt and O is out of scope; exempt and out-of-scope lines carry no VAT rate.
