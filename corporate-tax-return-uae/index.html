The short answer

A UAE Corporate Tax return is due no later than nine months after the end of the tax period, and any tax is due by the same date.

  • Year ended 31 December 2025: due 30 September 2026
  • Year ending 31 December 2026: due 30 September 2027
  • Filed on EmaraTax, even with no tax to pay
  • Late return: AED 500 a month, AED 1,000 from month 13
  • Late payment: 14% a year, charged monthly
  • Keep records for seven years

Example: A company with a 31 March 2026 year end files its return and pays by 31 December 2026.

Source: Federal Decree-Law No. 47 of 2022, Articles 48, 53 and 56; Cabinet Decision No. 75 of 2023 as amended.

The rules

Filing a Corporate Tax return.

Federal Decree-Law No. 47 of 2022, Cabinet Decision No. 75 of 2023 on penalties, and Ministry of Finance and FTA publications. Checked 12 November 2026.

Who files
Every taxable person files a return for each tax period, including those eligible for Small Business Relief. FTA: return and payment deadline notice, 26 September 2026 (opens in a new tab)
Return deadline
No later than nine months from the end of the tax period, or another date the FTA directs (Article 53). UAE Legislation: Federal Decree-Law No. 47 of 2022 (opens in a new tab)
Payment deadline
The Corporate Tax payable is settled within nine months from the end of the tax period (Article 48). UAE Legislation: Federal Decree-Law No. 47 of 2022 (opens in a new tab)
Tax period
The financial year: the calendar year, or the 12-month period the business prepares financial statements for (Article 57). A change needs an application to the FTA (Article 58). UAE Legislation: Federal Decree-Law No. 47 of 2022 (opens in a new tab)
Where to file
On EmaraTax, the FTA's online platform, directly or through a tax agent approved for Corporate Tax. FTA: return and payment deadline notice, 26 September 2026 (opens in a new tab)
What the return contains
At least: the tax period, the name, address and tax registration number, the accounting basis used, the taxable income, tax losses and tax credits claimed, and the Corporate Tax payable (Article 53). UAE Legislation: Federal Decree-Law No. 47 of 2022 (opens in a new tab)
Rates
0% on taxable income up to AED 375,000 and 9% above that. Ministry of Finance: Corporate Tax (opens in a new tab)
Small Business Relief
Resident businesses with revenue of AED 3 million or less can elect it in the return, for tax periods ending on or before 31 December 2029. A return is still filed. Ministry of Finance: Small Business Relief extended to 2029 (opens in a new tab)
Tax losses
A loss can be set against the taxable income of later periods, up to 75% of that income in any one period (Article 37). UAE Legislation: Federal Decree-Law No. 47 of 2022 (opens in a new tab)
Financial statements
Taxable income starts from standalone financial statements prepared under accounting standards accepted in the UAE (Article 20). UAE Legislation: Federal Decree-Law No. 47 of 2022 (opens in a new tab)
Audited financial statements
Required for a taxable person with revenue above AED 50 million in the tax period, and for every Qualifying Free Zone Person. A tax group prepares audited special purpose financial statements. Applies to tax periods starting on or after 1 January 2025. Ministry of Finance: Ministerial Decision No. 84 of 2025 (opens in a new tab)
Late return
AED 500 for each month or part of a month for the first twelve months, then AED 1,000 for each month or part of a month. It is imposed the day after the deadline and on the same date each month. FTA: Cabinet Decision No. 75 of 2023 as amended (unofficial translation) (opens in a new tab)
Late payment
A monthly penalty of 14% a year on the unpaid tax, for each month or part of a month, from the day after the due date. FTA: Cabinet Decision No. 75 of 2023 as amended (unofficial translation) (opens in a new tab)
Incorrect return
AED 500, unless the return is corrected before the filing deadline. FTA: Cabinet Decision No. 75 of 2023 as amended (unofficial translation) (opens in a new tab)
Records
Kept for seven years after the end of the tax period they relate to (Article 56). UAE Legislation: Federal Decree-Law No. 47 of 2022 (opens in a new tab)
Missing records
AED 10,000 for not keeping the required records, or AED 20,000 if repeated within 24 months. FTA: Cabinet Decision No. 75 of 2023 as amended (unofficial translation) (opens in a new tab)
Registered late?
The AED 10,000 late registration penalty is waived if the first return is filed within seven months of the end of the first tax period. FTA: Corporate Tax registration service (opens in a new tab)

Step by step

From closed books to a filed return.

  1. Close the books

    Finish the bookkeeping for the tax period and prepare standalone financial statements. They are the starting point for taxable income.

  2. Work out taxable income

    Start from the accounting income in the financial statements and make the adjustments the law requires. Set earlier tax losses against it, up to 75% of the income.

  3. Decide on Small Business Relief

    If revenue is AED 3 million or less, check whether you can and should elect the relief. The election is made in the return.

  4. File on EmaraTax

    Complete the Corporate Tax return on EmaraTax, directly or through an approved tax agent, no later than nine months after the period ends.

  5. Pay by the same date

    Settle any Corporate Tax payable by the return deadline.

  6. Keep the records

    Keep the financial statements and supporting records for seven years after the end of the tax period.

Questions

Common questions, answered.

When is the UAE Corporate Tax return due?

No later than nine months after the end of the tax period (Article 53 of Federal Decree-Law No. 47 of 2022). Any tax is due by the same date.

What is the deadline for a financial year ended 31 December 2025?

30 September 2026, for both the return and the payment, as the FTA confirmed in its notice of 26 September 2026.

What is the deadline for a financial year ending 31 December 2026?

30 September 2027: nine months after the end of the tax period.

Do I have to file if I made no profit or owe no tax?

Yes. Every taxable person files a return for each tax period, even when no tax is due.

Does Small Business Relief mean I do not file?

No. The relief is elected in the return, and the FTA requires those eligible for it to file within the same nine months.

What is the penalty for filing a Corporate Tax return late?

AED 500 for each month or part of a month for the first twelve months, then AED 1,000 for each month or part of a month (Cabinet Decision No. 75 of 2023 as amended).

What is the penalty for paying Corporate Tax late?

A monthly penalty of 14% a year on the unpaid tax, for each month or part of a month, from the day after the due date.

Do I need audited financial statements?

Yes if your revenue in the tax period is above AED 50 million, or if you are a Qualifying Free Zone Person (Ministerial Decision No. 84 of 2025).

How long must I keep Corporate Tax records?

Seven years after the end of the tax period they relate to (Article 56).
