How we work

A clear process, from first review to every filing.

  1. 01

    Diagnose

    We review your books, registrations and filing history, and give you a written status of what’s done, what’s due and what’s at risk.

  2. 02

    Set up

    We reconcile and clean up the ledger, align your chart of accounts, and complete any outstanding FTA registrations.

  3. 03

    Automate

    We connect document intake, bank feeds and approvals, so routine entries arrive pre-coded for review.

  4. 04

    Report & file

    Each month you get a closed ledger and an MIS pack. VAT and Corporate Tax returns are prepared and filed on schedule.
